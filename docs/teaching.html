I have served as a TA for microeconomics courses at the undergraduate and PhD levels. I was awarded the Northwestern Economics Department’s Distinguished TA Award for the 2022-2023 academic year, the only year in which I was eligible to receive it.

This table displays average scores from my teaching evaluations, as well as links to each evaluation. Across all categories and years, the average score is a 5.87 out of 6, and 89% of scores were a 6 out of 6.
